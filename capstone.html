Clinical Trial Biomarker Analytics & Advanced Data Visualization Pipelines

Healthcare Analytics | Custom Geometric Prototyping | Multi-Dimensional Data Visualization

Project Overview

An extensive semester-long exploration of clinical trial biomarkers and advanced graphical design principles. The project transitions from programmatic data exploration to hand-drawn geometric prototyping, culminating in dense, highly tailored multi-dimensional infographics engineered to communicate complex, nested healthcare relationships to technical and executive stakeholders.

Key Methodologies & Analytical Milestones

  • Programmatic Data Exploration & Categorization: Analyzed clinical trial trackers to evaluate scaling across Fluid, Imaging, and Digital biomarkers, mapping baseline totals across phase allocations and functional utilization metrics.
  • Geometric Design Prototyping: Ideated non-standard geometric visualizations—including Concentric Arch Profiles, Tiered Proportional Rings, and Categorized Proximity Bubbles—to overcome traditional bar and pie chart limitations.
  • Advanced Multi-Dimensional Infographics: Constructed Alluvial Flow Networks mapping trial targets into CADRO classifications, built Co-Occurrence Matrix Heatmaps for biological source cross-tabulation, and engineered custom Voronoi compartmental matrices for fluid tracking pipelines.
